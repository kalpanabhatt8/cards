Clemora is an elegant serif that blends classic sophistication with a modern touch. Inspired by the world of fashion and editorial design, Clemora delivers a feminine, sophisticated, and luxurious aesthetic, making it perfect for branding, weddings, logos, packaging, websites, and social media. With refined letterforms and a high-end look, Clemora is ideal for designers seeking a stylish, minimalist, and premium typeface. This font includes ligatures and unique letter alternates, allowing for creativity and exclusivity in every project.
